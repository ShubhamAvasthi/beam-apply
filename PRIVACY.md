# Privacy Policy

BeamApply is a browser extension that autofills job application forms with a
profile you enter. It is private by design, and this policy is short because
there is nothing to collect.

## What BeamApply stores

BeamApply stores the profile you enter — your name, contact details, work
history, pre-written answers, and an optional resume — **locally in your
browser**, using the browser's built-in extension storage. Nothing leaves
your device.

## How BeamApply uses it

When you click autofill on a job application page, BeamApply fills the
fields of that page with your saved profile. That is the only thing it does
with your data.

## What BeamApply does not do

- No data collection, transmission, or sharing — the extension makes no
  network requests at all.
- No servers, accounts, analytics, trackers, or advertising.
- No cookies.

## Data retention and deletion

Your profile stays on your device until you remove it. Delete everything
BeamApply has stored by uninstalling the extension, or by clearing the
profile on the extension's options page.

## Source code

BeamApply is open source (GPL-3.0). You can audit exactly what it does at
https://github.com/ShubhamAvasthi/beam-apply.
